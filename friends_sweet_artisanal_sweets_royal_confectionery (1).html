Our Story

A Legacy of Sweetness, Rooted in Assam

Friends Sweets & Bakery began as a small family kitchen in Dibrugarh, driven by one simple belief — that mithai should be made the honest way, with pure ghee, fresh milk, and patience.

What started as a humble neighbourhood confectionery has grown into a name families across Assam trust for weddings, festivals, and everyday celebrations. Every batch is still prepared by hand each morning, following recipes refined over generations, using nothing but pure desi ghee, fresh mawa, and carefully sourced dry fruits. No shortcuts, no synthetic flavors — just the same warmth and craft our founders started with.
